Strategic Insights

  • The sequence moves from curiosity-driven opener → credibility-building midpoint → urgency-based close without ever feeling pushy.
  • Every CTA offers an escape hatch: Paul can engage with zero commitment, request content without a call, or opt into a short working session.
  • The Linear case study is deliberately chosen: it signals "I understand devtools" and creates subtle competitive FOMO without naming a direct Supabase rival.

Recommendations

  • Track Email 3 open rates closely — breakup emails often see the highest engagement because the psychological pressure to respond drops.
